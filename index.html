    How to get the token on iPhone
    1. Tap Copy bookmarklet.
    2. In Safari, bookmark any page (Share, Add Bookmark), then Bookmarks, Edit, open that bookmark and replace its address with the copied text; name it "LR token".
    3. Open languagereactor.com in Safari and log in.
    4. Open Bookmarks and tap "LR token": a box shows your email; tap Copy for LR Reader.
    5. Back here: Paste Language Reactor login, then Save.
    The bookmarklet only asks Language Reactor for your own token, using the login already in Safari.

    Language Reactor is still used for your account: saved words, highlighting, saving words and phrases.

    More Spanish voices: iOS Settings > Accessibility > Spoken Content > Voices > Spanish (Enhanced and Premium sound best).
